About Our Faculty

Our faculty consists of experienced educators, researchers, and industry professionals dedicated to academic excellence and student success. They guide students through classroom learning, practical training, research activities, and career development.

Faculty Responsibilities

  • Delivering high-quality classroom instruction.
  • Preparing and updating course materials.
  • Conducting practical and laboratory sessions.
  • Mentoring and guiding students.
  • Evaluating assignments and examinations.
  • Maintaining academic records.
  • Supporting research and innovation.
  • Encouraging ethical and professional values.
  • Assisting students with academic concerns.
  • Participating in institutional development.
