Registration Guidelines

Before starting your registration, please ensure that:

  • You have a valid email address and stable internet connection.
  • You have all required documents ready (clear and readable).
  • All information entered is accurate.
  • You complete all mandatory fields marked with an asterisk (*).
  • You do not refresh or close the browser during registration.

Security & Passwords

Password Guidelines

  • Use at least 8 characters.
  • Include uppercase and lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters.
  • Never share your password with anyone.
  • Change your password periodically.

Security Tips

  • Never share your OTP.
  • Always log out after using a public computer.
  • Verify the website address before logging in.
  • Report suspicious activity immediately.

Technical Support

If you encounter technical issues (Login problems, Registration errors, Document upload failures, OTP issues, or Dashboard loading problems), please contact the system administrator with:

  • Your registered email address
  • A brief description of the issue
  • A screenshot of the error (if available)
